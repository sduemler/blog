### ALRIGHT MAN. It’s time to talk about The Yard Podcast.

Started by the streamer Ludwig and his then roommates and still friends Nick, Aiden, and Slime, it has been a mainstay in my media consumption since they debuted on July 8th, 2021. We were still in the throes of the pandemic, and seeing 4 friends around my age talk about the things that I like was like a drug to my brain.

Since then, I have subscribed to the $25 tier of their Patreon, where they host extra content and shows. I own \_\_\_ shirts, \_\_\_ hoodies, 2 hats, 1 pair of shorts, 3 pairs of underwear, 1 cardigan, 7 postcards, and 1 book. It is my goal to eventually be able to go out in a full Yard outfit, currently all I’m missing are the socks and shoes.

All of this to say - I’m a fan.

## Parasocialite

There have been a few period where life gets busy and I lose track of time, but for most of the weeks from the past 3 years Thursday has always been Yard day for me. The main episode gets released on YouTube about midday for me, so whenever I get back from the gym on Thursday it’s usually Yard and lunch time. Then later in the day is the Patreon bonus episode and dinner time. Sometimes it’ll last me through Friday, but more often than not I run through it all on the day it gets released.

Whenever I feel myself getting a little too dependent or parasocial I try to take a week off. It helps that I’m not on Twitter (but [here’s](https://chocobosam.com/post/tweets/) what I would say if I was), so I’m not following their every thought and word. I do catch a Slime or Ludwig stream every once in a while if they are costreaming a melee tournament or if I’m playing a game that doesn’t take a lot of attention and they happen to be streaming at the same time.

One thing that I’ll always be thankful to the Yard for is their openness on talking about emotional situations. There have been two specific episodes that have been a standout in that, both centered around Aiden. The first was a main episode where Aiden gets pushed to talk about his performance at a recent melee tournament, and he breaks down and cries when talking about how hard he tries and how he feels that others don’t believe in it. It felt like a rare moment of emotional vulnerability among a swath of comedy podcasts who would have clowned the shit out of someone for that, or at the very least cut it out from the final product. But all the other members let him talk about it, and comforted him and reminded him that they were all friends and believed in him. blah blah edit here. Now they *did* edit Subway Surfers and soap cutting into the portion where Aiden is breaking down, but this was at Aiden’s request to make it funnier. The strange thing was it didn’t take away from the emotional impact of it, it just had a pure add of comedy instead of lessening the effect.

Then talk about the Aiden only Patreon episode.

The other aspect that has helped me emotionally has been how forthwith Ludwig and Slime have been about losing their fathers. My own dad died in August of 2023. About 6 months before then I had read Slime’s writing about his own father passing, and afterwards I had to go sit down in the shower and cry for about 45 minutes. I have wanted to read it again after losing my dad, but every time I pull it up I only get through about the first paragraph before I can’t read the words through the tears anymore. Slime is a fantastic writer and you can read it here if you’re interested: [Eulogy for a Gambler](https://medium.com/@slimewire/eulogy-for-a-gambler-5985e5c659e3).

They have also been very open with how they coped with the massive loss in the days after. Ludwig said that he just watched cartoons for 3 months straight afterwards. Slime said that he grinded all the data battles in Kingdom Hearts for the 5 months after. I have been somewhat self-conscious about how long it’s taken me to get back to what feels like normal after losing my dad about a year ago now. There have been other extenuating circumstances that I think have extended that time for me, but it made me feel some better about hermitizing myself for months afterwards and losing all forward momentum in the things that I liked to do.

## Comfort Club

To me, The Yard is my comfort show. I’ve been through all the episodes at least twice, and am currently working through rewatching all the Patreon episodes as well. I’ve heard from others that they will throw on Friends or The Office in the background of whatever they’re doing at home lke folding laundry or washing the dishes. For me that is the Yard. I rarely just sit down to watch the podcast
